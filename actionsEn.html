Our Actions

Our past events

Throughout the year, we organized several initiatives aimed at raising awareness of environmental issues among young people. These events allowed us to bring people together, take concrete action, and showcase the creativity of our team.

Our project

We are currently developing several initiatives aimed at strengthening environmental commitment within our community. These include the creation of interactive workshops to raise awareness among young people about sustainable practices, as well as the establishment of local partnerships to organize concrete actions on the ground.

In parallel, we are working on new communication tools to make information more accessible and engaging. These projects are designed to encourage participation, inspire change, and allow everyone to contribute, at their own level, to environmental protection.

Our upcoming projects

In the coming months, we plan to launch new campaigns focused on innovation and citizen participation. These will aim to reach an even wider audience and offer diverse opportunities for involvement, ranging from local actions to collaborative online projects.

We are also developing new events designed to promote the creativity of young people and their ideas for a more sustainable future. These future projects demonstrate our commitment to continue to evolve, adapt, and offer increasingly inspiring solutions for working together.
